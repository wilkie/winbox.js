A Windows 3.1 program built to use a coprocessor issues the 387's own instructions. Calculator does, for every number it shows. The recordings can't serve as the reference here: they come from Windows under DOSBox, whose FPU is a model that holds values in 64-bit doubles, not hardware. So winbox.js's FPU follows Intel's description of the instruction set, and a test assembles each instruction and runs it on the 386 core.

- [[documented]] Eight registers form a stack. `ST(i)` is the register `i` places below `TOP`, which is kept in bits 11 to 13 of the status word, so pushing lowers `TOP` and popping raises it. Every register operand is addressed from `TOP`, never by its physical number.
- [[documented]] With `ST(i)` as the destination, as `DC` and `DE` encode it, the subtraction and division come with their senses swapped. `DE E9` is `FSUBP ST(1),ST`, which is ST(1) − ST(0). `DE E1` is `FSUBRP ST(1),ST`, which is ST(0) − ST(1).
- [[documented]] Comparisons set `C3 C2 C0`: 000 when ST(0) is greater, 001 when it is less, 100 when they are equal, and 111 when either is a NaN. A program reads them with `FNSTSW AX`.
- [[documented]] `FIST`, `FRNDINT` and `FBSTP` round as the control word's bits 10 and 11 say: to nearest, with a half going to the even integer, or down, up, or toward zero. `FINIT` leaves the control word `037Fh`, which rounds to nearest and masks every exception. A value that doesn't fit is stored as the integer indefinite, the most negative integer of the size, with `IE` set.
- [[documented]] `FPREM` leaves the three low bits of its quotient in `C0`, `C3` and `C1`. `FXAM` classifies ST(0) in `C3 C2 C0` (zero, normal, infinity, NaN or empty) with its sign in `C1`.
- Held as doubles: an 80-bit value in memory loads and stores exactly whenever a double can hold it. One with more precision than that is rounded to nearest on the way in. Exceptions are recorded in the status word, as masked ones are, and never raised.
- [[documented]] The precision exception, 20h, is recorded where a result is not the exact one. Here that is where the unit's double is not: a sum, difference, product or quotient, a square root, `FSCALE`, `FRNDINT`, and an integer or a single stored. Each is told exactly. A sum's error is itself a double and is computed. A product is the product of the operands' odd parts, which a 128-bit integer holds, and so are a quotient times its divisor and a root squared. An overflow is inexact too. The loads and the constants gather none, as the 387's do not.
- [[documented]] A NaN made from operands that are not NaNs is an invalid operation: nought by nought, infinity by infinity, nought times infinity, and infinities of the two signs added. Nought by nought is invalid, not a division by nought.

## With a coprocessor or without

A program built for floating point is written for the coprocessor. Each of its instructions comes with an `FWAIT` before it and an OS fixup, a relocation of its own kind. KERNEL applies the fixups as it loads the program, and what it writes depends on whether there is a coprocessor. winbox.js's machine has one by default, as the Windows the recordings are made on does, and the run page can take it away.

- [[measured]] [[probe:winflags]] finds a coprocessor on that Windows. `GetWinFlags` and `__WINFLAGS` carry it as 400h. See [[fn:KERNEL.GetWinFlags]].
- [[read out]] With a coprocessor, KERNEL makes each `FWAIT` a `NOP`, and the instruction runs on the unit (`KRNL386.EXE` seg1 `7536`, tables at `74f3`). Without one, it makes the instruction `INT 34h` to `3Bh`, one for each ESC opcode `D8h` to `DFh`. An instruction with a segment prefix becomes `INT 3Ch`, followed by a byte whose top two bits name the segment: DS, SS, CS or ES. A lone `FWAIT` becomes `INT 3Dh` either way. Every instruction keeps its length.
- [[read out]] `__FPMATH` 0 sets the handlers (`WIN87EM.DLL` seg1 `ff`). Without a coprocessor, `INT 34h` to `3Bh` go to the emulator at `d0a`, `INT 3Ch` to `cca` and `INT 3Dh` to `cc0`, each a byte on in enhanced mode, past its `STI`. With one, they go to `8d4`, `840` and `86d`.
- [[read out]] The emulator takes the ESC opcode from the interrupt's number, `D8h` on. For `INT 3Ch` it takes the byte after it, with its top two bits made ones, and those bits name the operand's segment: DS, SS, CS or ES (`cca`-`d05`, the table at `cc2`). It reads the ModR/M byte and the displacement after it (the table at `920`), carries the instruction out, and returns past it. It never writes the instruction back. [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]] reads a function's first bytes after it has run, and they are the fixups' `CD 39`, `CD 38` and `CD 3D`.
- [[read out]] A lone `FWAIT`, `INT 3Dh`, returns and does nothing else (`cc0`).
- [[read out]] What it carries out (the tables at `960`-`9d7`). The even opcodes' memory forms are the arithmetic and comparisons on a value loaded. Their register forms are too, after a look for `ST(i)` on the stack (`e81`): `DA`'s are run as `D8`'s and then popped, `DE`'s comparisons as `D8`'s and popped. The odd opcodes' memory forms are the loads and stores of each size, `FLDCW`, `FNSTCW` and `FNSTSW`. `FLDENV`, `FNSTENV`, `FRSTOR`, `FNSAVE`, `FBLD`, `FBSTP` and the reserved forms are unemulated. Their register forms go by `reg` whatever the opcode:
  - `FLD ST(i)` and `FXCH`, `D9`'s; `DB`'s, `DD`'s and `DF`'s first group is `FFREE`, which takes the register out and closes the stack up over it (`2115`).
  - `FST ST(i)` for `DB`, `DD` and `DF`, and `FSTP ST(i)` for `DD`. `D9 D0`, `FNOP`, is unemulated.
  - `FCHS`, `FABS`, `FTST` and `FXAM` for `D9`, and `FNSTSW AX` for `DF E0` (`f6f`-`f98`). `DB E0`, `E2`, `E4` and `E6` and `DF E2`, `E4` and `E6` clear the status word, `FNCLEX` among them. `FNINIT` is unemulated.
  - The seven constants, and `F2XM1`, `FYL2X`, `FPTAN`, `FPATAN`, `FPREM`, `FYL2XP1`, `FSQRT`, `FRNDINT` and `FSCALE`, for any of the odd opcodes. `FXTRACT`, `FPREM1`, `FDECSTP`, `FINCSTP`, `FSINCOS`, `FSIN` and `FCOS` are unemulated.
- [[read out]] What it does not carry out it passes over and marks with 40h (`ffd`). Each instruction's exceptions are gathered in a word (`ec0`). Its low byte goes into the status word, and the whole into the exceptions `__FPMATH` 8 answers. 2000h marks an operand in memory and is never reported. The status word has no TOP, so a stored one has nought there. [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]] finds `FSTSW` after `FLD1` storing 0120h: TOP nought, `C0`, and the precision exception gathered from the instructions before.
- [[read out]] `FLDCW` sets the program's control word and the one the arithmetic uses to the same value (`1f84`). `FNSTCW` stores the program's (`1f8d`). [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]]'s `FSTCW` stores 1332h, the control word `__FPMATH` started.
- [[read out]] The exceptions the program's control word leaves unmasked are reported (`ecd`-`ede`). A denormal is always masked. The unemulated, the square root of a negative and the stack's faults never are. The procedure at `INT 3Eh`'s vector is called with the code in AL (`67c`-`6bb`): 8Bh for the stack underflowing, 8Ah overflowing, 88h the square root of a negative, 89h for a bit 100h whose setter is not followed here, 81h invalid, 83h a division by nought, 84h overflow, 85h underflow, 86h precision, 87h unemulated. AH is the program's BH, as the code is made in BL and exchanged into AX. WIN87EM reads the vector and calls it whatever it holds (`6cf` jumps to the next instruction either way), with its own data segment as DS (`6eb`-`72c`). When it returns, the program goes on past the instruction.
- [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]] is recorded without a coprocessor: `record.mjs` clears the BIOS's equipment word's coprocessor bit before Windows starts, and KERNEL takes the bit from there (`KRNL386.EXE` seg1 `aa67`). It finds 87h for `FNOP`, `FNINIT` and `FLDENV`, 83h for 1 / 0, 88h for the square root of -1, and the program going on past each. With BX 1200h, AX was 1287h.
- [[read out]] With a coprocessor, `INT 34h` to `3Ch` are not made by KERNEL, but WIN87EM still answers them. It writes the instruction back as `FWAIT` and the ESC opcode, or `FWAIT`, the segment's prefix and the opcode for `INT 3Ch`, and returns to it (`8d4`, `840`). The `FWAIT` becomes a `NOP`, except before `FNSTENV`, `FNSTCW`, `FNSAVE`, `FNSTSW`, `FNCLEX`, `FNINIT` and `FNSTSW AX` (`8ec`-`917`).
- [[read out]] `__FPMATH`, called with its function in BX, starts and resets the unit. Its control word is 1332h, and the unit is given 1330h, with invalid operations and denormals unmasked. It sets and reads the control word, rounds, pops a long, reads and clears the gathered exceptions, counts the stack, and says whether there is a coprocessor. Calculator asks, and with a 387 uses `FSIN` and `FCOS` directly.
- [[read out]] WIN87EM's arithmetic rounds every result to the precision the control word's bits 8 and 9 name, 64 bits under 1332h, and gathers 20h where that rounding drops bits (seg1 `1576`-`1609`). A store rounds again and gathers 20h: as a single (`1796`), as a double (`19e4`), as an integer (`1df2`). [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]]'s 20h comes from the square root of 2 or from `FISTP` of pi, both before its `FSTSW`.
- [[read out]] WIN87EM's division takes nought by nought as a division by nought as well as an invalid operation, 04h and 01h (seg1 `1081`, from the table at `a48`, by the two operands' kinds). The 387 takes it as invalid alone. [[measured]] [[probe:fptask]]'s second program gathers 0005h from nought by nought.
- WinBox's emulator decodes each instruction as WIN87EM does and carries it out on the processor's own unit, the machine's, the one a coprocessor would be. Those it carries out as others it gives the unit as those. A stack fault the unit records is reported as WIN87EM reports one, an invalid `FSQRT` as the square root of a negative, and an invalid division of nought by nought with 04h. [[measured]] [[probe:fpemu]] agrees.

### One program and another

- [[read out]] Without a coprocessor, the floating-point state is the machine's. WIN87EM keeps it in its one data segment (`WIN87EM.DLL` seg2, 170h bytes, the emulator's stack among it). It imports nothing that names a task: KERNEL's ordinals 171 and 176 and `__WINFLAGS`. Its `__FPMATH` 0, which each program's runtime calls as it starts, resets it all (seg1 `44`, `b3`): the stack emptied, the control word 1332h, the gathered exceptions cleared. `__FPMATH` 2, as a program ends, does the same (`9f`).
- [[read out]] With a coprocessor, KERNEL's task switch keeps a task's control word, and nothing else of the unit. As a task gives the processor up, `FSTCW` stores the word in its database at 14h (`KRNL386.EXE` seg1 `8216`-`8220`). As a task takes the processor, `FNCLEX` clears the exceptions and `FLDCW` loads its word (`818b`-`8195`). Both happen only where SYSTEM's `Get80x87SaveSize` answered a size, kept at KERNEL's `[23Ch]` (`b6cc`). A new task's database is made noughts (seg2 `2154`-`215e`), so its word is nought until its runtime's `FNINIT`. The unit's registers are the machine's.
- [[measured]] [[probe:fptask]], without a coprocessor, sets its own control word (173Fh), gathers a division by nought and leaves 7 on the stack, then starts a second program. Once that program has started, the first sees 1332h, no exceptions and an empty stack. When the second sets 0F3Fh and gathers nought by nought, the first sees 0F3Fh and 0005h. When the second ends, the first sees 1332h.
- [[measured]] [[probe:fptsk87]], the same with a coprocessor, keeps each program's control word, 173Fh for the first and 0F3Fh for the second. The first's 7 is gone once the second has started, its runtime's `FNINIT` emptying the shared unit. It divides nothing, as DOSBox's unit gathers no exceptions: [[probe:fpemu]]'s first recording.
- WinBox keeps a task's control word as KERNEL does, with a coprocessor, and nothing more. The unit and WIN87EM's state are the machine's.

## In winbox.js

`crates/winbox-cpu/src/x87.rs` has the unit, and its `escape` runs an instruction whose opcode and ModR/M byte the emulator has read. `crates/winbox-win16/src/win87em.rs` is WIN87EM: `__FPMATH`, the emulator (`emulate`), the coprocessor's writing back (`put_back`) and the report (`report`). `run.rs` sends `INT 34h` to `3Ch` there. The machine's `coprocessor` option chooses, and the run page's switch sets it. `scheduler.rs` keeps each task's control word. The first version indexed the physical registers where the 387 addresses them from `TOP`. It never read the control word's rounding, and it threw on most of the `D9`, `DB`, `DD` and `DF` groups. Calculator stopped at the first of those it reached.

## Not yet done

- WIN87EM's own arithmetic, whose rounding and NaNs may differ from the unit's doubles, is not followed. Neither is its stack of fourteen values, where the unit has eight: a ninth value overflows here and not there.
- The checks each of WIN87EM's routines makes of the stack are followed only for `ST(i)` (`e81`). `FCHS` on an empty stack, for one, changes nothing there and is a stack fault here.
- `INT 3Eh`'s procedure is called from USER's callback thunk with the registers as they were, and they are all put back after. WIN87EM calls it with its own data segment in DS and puts back only AX, DS and BP. A vector never set is not called here. Windows' own default vector is not recorded, and [[probe:fpemu]]'s runtime sets one first.
- With a coprocessor, exceptions the unit raises are not reported, since it never raises them.
- The precision exception is told against the unit's doubles. WIN87EM and the 387 hold 64 bits of significand, and round to a double only as a value is stored as one. So a result exact in 64 bits but not in 53 gathers 20h here at the instruction, and there only once it is stored as a double. One never stored so gathers it here alone. And the other way: pi, a long integer or an eighty-bit value that a double cannot hold is rounded here as it is loaded, gathering nothing, so storing it as a double later gathers nothing here, where the 387 gathers 20h at the store. [[measured]] [[probe:sysdrv]]'s `FLDPI` gathers nothing under DOSBox.
- The transcendental instructions, `F2XM1`, `FYL2X`, `FYL2XP1`, `FPTAN`, `FPATAN`, `FSIN`, `FCOS` and `FSINCOS`, never gather 20h here. Their results come from the host's library, not rounded correctly, so whether one rounded can't be told.
- `C1`, which the 387 sets where a result was rounded up, is not set. Overflow and underflow gather 20h but not 08h or 10h.
- An ESC instruction without an `FWAIT`, which the compiler gives no fixup (`FNSTSW`, `FNSTCW`, the runtime's own `FNINIT`), is never an interrupt and runs on the unit, the one the emulator uses here. Under DOSBox it runs on DOSBox's own unit, apart from WIN87EM's state: [[probe:fpemu]]'s first recording, with `FNSTSW` and `FNSTCW`, stored 0000h and 127Fh there. What a machine with no coprocessor at all does with one is not recorded.

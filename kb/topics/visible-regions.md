**Status: complete** (GDI keeps the region, USER works it out, 2026-10-26; drawing goes through it alone, USER's entries and the hook, 2026-10-27; scrolling, moving and locking through it, 2026-10-27). What is left is under "Not yet done". The first half of this page is what Windows does, read out and recorded. The second half is how winbox.js is to do the same, in phases, each mergeable on its own, and the owner's decisions on it.

A window's device context draws only where the window shows. In Windows 3.1 that is two modules' work. USER works out which pixels of the screen a window's context may draw on -- its **visible region** -- whenever it hands out a context and whenever windows move, and gives it to GDI with [[fn:GDI.SelectVisRgn]] and [[fn:GDI.SetDCOrg]]. GDI keeps it in the context beside the program's own clip region ([[topic:clip-regions]]), and clips everything it draws to both. GDI exports a family of calls for USER to do this with; programs can call them too. winbox.js did it another way until the design below: each pixel of the screen knew which window showed there, and a window's context was a view of the screen that drew only on its window's pixels ([[topic:painting]]). That left the family as stubs, [[fn:USER.GetDCEx]]'s `DCX_PARENTCLIP` undone, and the clip box of a covered window wrong.

## In GDI

### What a device context keeps

[[read out]] Besides the fields of [[topic:device-context-internals]], three regions and an origin (`GDI.EXE` seg1):

| Offset       | What                                                                                                                        |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `0Eh`        | The clip region a program set, or none ([[fn:GDI.GetClipRgn]] answers it). In the screen's terms: moved by the origin.      |
| `1Eh`        | What drawing is clipped to: the visible region where there is no clip region, else a region of its own, the two intersected |
| `A0h`        | The visible region, in the screen's terms. Never none.                                                                      |
| `A2h`, `A4h` | The origin: where the context's (0, 0) is on the screen ([[fn:GDI.GetDCOrg]])                                               |
| `70h`, `72h` | The origin with the viewport's origin added, which maps a logical point to the device                                       |

- [[read out]] `1Eh` is made again (seg1 `17b1`) whenever either region changes: the visible region's handle itself where there is no clip region, else a region of the context's own, `RGN_AND` of the two. Its kind is what the calls answer (seg1 `1892`: 1 empty, 2 a rectangle, 3 more).
- [[read out]] The state that [[fn:GDI.SaveDC]] and [[fn:GDI.GetDCState]] copy is the first A0h bytes of the context, the origin and the clip region (seg1 `0a46`): **not the visible region**. When the visible region changes, every saved level is marked (seg1 `081d`, bit 4 of `0Ah`), and restoring one does not bring an old visible region back.
- [[read out]] [[fn:GDI.SelectClipRgn]] copies the region and moves it by the origin (seg1 `15f9`-`161a`); [[fn:GDI.IntersectClipRect]] and [[fn:GDI.ExcludeClipRect]] with no clip region start from the whole surface, the bitmap's or the display's `HORZRES` by `VERTRES` from (0, 0) (seg1 `1722`-`1752`).

### The calls

All of these first call the context's hook where its flag is set ([[topic:device-context-internals]]), then:

- [[fn:GDI.SelectVisRgn]] (seg1 `1635`): the region's bytes copied into the context's own visible region (seg1 `58b3`), the saved levels marked, `1Eh` made again; answers its kind. [[measured]] The region given stays the program's: changed after, the context's is not ([[probe:visfam]]).
- [[fn:GDI.IntersectVisRect]] and [[fn:GDI.ExcludeVisRect]] (seg1 `16a2`, `16a5`): one body with `IntersectClipRect` and `ExcludeClipRect` (`16b9`), told apart by the field they change (`A0h` or `0Eh`). The rectangle is **in the screen's terms and not mapped**. [[measured]] The wrong way round, `IntersectVisRect` leaves nothing and answers 1.
- [[fn:GDI.OffsetVisRgn]] (seg1 `2929`): moves the visible region, answers the kind.
- [[fn:GDI.SaveVisRgn]] (seg1 `076c`): a copy of the visible region pushed on a stack kept in the region's own first word; answers the copy that was on top before. [[measured]] The first answers nought, the second a handle.
- [[fn:GDI.RestoreVisRgn]] (seg1 `07b2`): the copy on top becomes the visible region, the old one deleted; answers **the visible region's** kind (`0811`), where the others answer that of `1Eh` (`17b1`). [[measured]] With nothing saved it answers −1 and changes nothing. After a restore, [[fn:GDI.InquireVisRgn]] answers another handle than before.
- [[fn:GDI.InquireVisRgn]] (seg1 `200a`): the handle at `A0h` -- the visible region itself, not a copy. [[measured]] The same handle each time until a restore.
- [[fn:GDI.SetDCOrg]] (seg1 `1118`): the origin exchanged, the clip region moved by the difference; **the visible region stays where it is**. Answers the old origin, x in the low word.
- [[fn:GDI.GetDCState]] and [[fn:GDI.SetDCState]] (seg1 `0601`, `0615`): a copy of the state as `SaveDC` makes one, as a handle; and that state copied back, the origin set through `SetDCOrg` where it differs, the visible region kept. [[measured]] The text colour comes back; a visible region narrowed between stays narrowed.
- [[fn:GDI.FastWindowFrame]] (seg1 `420e`): a rectangle's frame, `xWidth` across and `yWidth` down, drawn in one call by the display driver's `FastBorder` (the driver's table at 40h) -- only on a screen context whose driver has `RC_GDI20_OUTPUT` and whose clip is one rectangle; nought otherwise, and USER then draws the frame another way (`USER.EXE` seg1 `8d74`-`8d7d`). [[measured]] On a window's context it answers −1 and draws the frame; with a corner cut out of the visible region it answers nought and draws nothing; on a memory context, nought.
- [[fn:GDI.Death]] and [[fn:GDI.Resurrection]] (seg1 `2067`, `208e`): the display driver's `Disable` and `Enable`, through the context's driver; `Resurrection` sets the palette again on a palette device. USER calls them as it gives the screen to a full-screen DOS program and takes it back ([[topic:user-internals]]). Not recorded: under the recorder they would turn the display off. [[read out]] `Disable` answers −1 on the VGA (`VGA.DRV` seg4 `0274`), the 256-colour Super VGA (`SVGA256.DRV` seg3 `0000`), the EGA, the Super VGA and the Hercules, whatever the state; nought on the XGA (`XGA.DRV` seg3 `0900`, DPMI's "get CPU mode" kept from its start); on the 8514/a (`8514.DRV` seg6 `03b7`) what AX holds as its hook on INT 2Fh comes off -- 252Fh while the hook is in, **a state**: a second call with no `Enable` between leaves what the BIOS left, not measured. `Enable` answers 1 once the mode is set, on each (`VGA.DRV` seg4 `02b2`, `SVGA256.DRV` seg3 `07fc`, `8514.DRV` seg6 `030e`, `XGA.DRV` seg3 `0755`). Their AX is what `Death` and `Resurrection` answer.
- [[read out]] [[fn:GDI.SelectObject]] of a bitmap into a memory context sets its visible region to the bitmap's rectangle from (0, 0) (seg1 `1cd8`-`1cf7`, `SetRectRgn` on the region at A0h).

### Drawing and asking

- [[read out]] Every drawing call clips to `1Eh`. A blit walks its rectangles, calling the driver once for each that meets the destination (seg1 `2e74`-`2ec3`).
- [[read out]] [[fn:GDI.GetClipBox]] is the box of `1Eh`, mapped back to logical terms (seg1 `1831`); [[fn:GDI.RectVisible]] and [[fn:GDI.PtVisible]] ask `1Eh` (seg1 `29b7`, `2b0e`). [[measured]] So a window covered in part answers a clip box of what shows of it, and kind 3 ([[probe:visrgn]]).
- [[measured]] [[fn:GDI.GetClipRgn]]'s region is in the screen's terms: `SelectClipRgn` of (0, 0, 20, 20) on a context at (100, 100) gives back (100, 100, 120, 120), and it moves with `SetDCOrg`.

## In USER

### Contexts USER hands out

[[read out]] USER keeps a list of entries, each a context and what it was got for (`USER.EXE` data `12Eh`; each entry, 16 bytes from `LocalAlloc` (seg1 `88d6`): the next at 0, the context at 2, the window at 6, the window whose region it has at 8, a region given at 0Ah, the flags at 0Ch and 0Eh). Five are made as Windows starts, for common contexts, each `CreateDC("DISPLAY")` with no window and the flags `DCX_CACHE` and 800h (seg3 `0635`-`0662`, seg1 `88aa`); each new entry goes to the head of the list, and has USER's `DCHook` set on its context with the entry as the hook's data (`890d`-`8917`). A context of a `CS_OWNDC` window is an entry of its own, made as the window is created; a `CS_CLASSDC` class's, as its first window is (seg8 `0707`-`071e`) -- found by its window or by the class's context (seg1 `8560`-`85b3`), in use from the start and stale, so that its region is worked out as it is first used (`896d`-`8972`). A copy of a new context's state is kept with `GetDCState` (seg3 `0665`) for giving a common context back.

[[read out]] USER's own flags beside `GetDCEx`'s: 800h an entry emptied, its region to be worked out when next taken; 1000h in use; 2000h made stale while in use (`8698`, `8b6e`, `8ba0`). An entry is matched by its window whose region it has and the flags `DCX_WINDOW`, `DCX_CACHE`, `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN`, `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` and `DCX_LOCKWINDOWUPDATE`, with neither 800h nor 1000h set (`854f`, `85cd`).

`GetDCEx`'s body (seg1 `847b`) takes the flags:

- [[read out]] Before the body, `GetDCEx` checks its arguments (`a64c`): a window that is none, or a region given that is not a region (GDI's object type, not 6), answers nought (`a658`-`a66d`) -- 1 too, which the body takes for the whole screen and only USER's own callers pass; flags with bits it does not know (`a676`: FB04h of the low word, all but bit 0 of the high) are a warning, and it goes on. [[measured]] [[probe:visrule]]: `GetDCEx` with 1 and `DCX_EXCLUDERGN` or `DCX_INTERSECTRGN` answers nought.
- [[read out]] With `DCX_USESTYLE` (10000h), which [[fn:USER.GetDC]], [[fn:USER.GetWindowDC]] and [[fn:USER.BeginPaint]] pass, the clipping flags given are dropped (`8497`) and taken from the styles: for `DCX_WINDOW`, `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` from `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` and `DCX_CACHE`, nothing else (`84f1`-`84fb`); for the client area, the class's `CS_PARENTDC` gives `DCX_PARENTCLIP`, a class with neither `CS_OWNDC` nor `CS_CLASSDC` gives `DCX_CACHE`, the window's `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` gives `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` (not while it is minimized, `84e5`), its `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` (`84a1`-`84ef`). A bit of the high word, 8 (80000h, no name in the SDK), drops `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` and `DCX_PARENTCLIP` and gives `DCX_CACHE` (`84ff`).
- [[read out]] `DCX_PARENTCLIP` (`850d`-`854c`) gives `DCX_CACHE`, and the parent's region is used, with the parent's `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` as `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` and without `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN`, unless the parent has `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` (not asked where a bit of the window's own, bit 2 of its word at 26h, is set: `851a`), the flags have `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` or `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS`, or one is visible and the other not. A window at the top's parent is the desktop, which has `WS_CLIPCHILDREN`. The origin stays the child's, and whether the window shows, and the lock, are asked of the child (`86b2`-`86bc`: seg1 `1582` is given both). [[measured]] [[probe:visrule]]: a child of a parent with `WS_CLIPCHILDREN`, one given `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` or `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` too, and a hidden child of a shown parent each get their own region; with `DCX_WINDOW`, the parent's window rectangle.
- [[read out]] A common context is an entry not in use for the same window whose region it has and the same flags, if there is one -- **taken as it is, its region not worked out again**, its origin set again only where it was another window's (`85c5`-`85e8`, `8656`-`866f`); else the first emptied entry, or the last free one (`85f4`-`8619`). Where all five are in use USER logs an error (43h); where an entry's window's word at 18h is not what seg1 `817b` answers -- [[inferred]] a window of another task's -- it yields (KERNEL's `Yield`) and looks again; else it gives back the entry taken last and takes it again (`8625`-`8683`). A taken entry's region is worked out (`86b2`-`86c8`, below), given with `SelectVisRgn`, and its origin and brush origin set from the window (seg1 `1540`). The entry taken goes to the head of the list (`86d1`-`86e3`).
- [[measured]] [[probe:dcentry]] records which context comes, by whether two handles are the same: a window's given back and asked for again is the same; `BeginPaint`'s and a `GetDC` inside the paint are two, and the `GetDC` after the paint is the one inside it; five windows asked for in turn are five contexts, a sixth window's the first's, and the first's after it the second's; `GetWindowDC` another than `GetDC`, and `GetDC` after it `GetDC`'s again; six of the screen's held at once are five, the sixth the fifth again, and a window's asked for then the fifth too -- given back, the first five answer 1 and the same handle again nought; a `CS_OWNDC` window's is the same each time and keeps its text colour, given back or not, and is none of the five; a `CS_CLASSDC` class's two windows' are the same; a common one lent again has its text colour, mapping mode and background mode as new. [[probe:dcreuse]] the same of the screen's against windows'.
- [[read out]] `DCX_INTERSECTRGN` and `DCX_EXCLUDERGN` (`86e7`-`87a0`): `SaveVisRgn`, then the visible region combined with the region given, in the screen's terms, and selected. [[fn:USER.BeginPaint]] passes the window's update region this way (seg1 `7671`, with `DCX_USESTYLE`), and the erase its own (`7ac6`).
- [[read out]] [[fn:USER.ReleaseDC]] (`8805`): the entry found by its context alone, the window not asked; nought where it is not in use or was emptied (`881e`-`8825`). Where a region was given and the entry is a common one or `EndPaint` gives it back (`ReleaseDC`'s 1, seg1 `76d4`), `RestoreVisRgn` and the region deleted unless `DCX_KEEPCLIPRGN` (seg1 `885f`); a common context then has its state set back with `SetDCState` from the copy kept at the start, and is no longer in use (`883e`-`884e`). A window's own context and a class's stay in use, and answer 1. [[measured]] The clip region goes, but **the visible region and origin stay** -- a program's `SelectVisRgn` or `SetDCOrg` is still there when the same window gets the same entry again ([[probe:visfam]], `again`, `origin`): `SetDCState` copies the origin back and sets it again through `SetDCOrg` to what it was (`GDI.EXE` seg1 `072f`-`073f`).
- [[read out]] A window destroyed (seg8 `0d90`-`0e05`): its common entries in use given back, and each emptied; a class's emptied, its region given restored; a window's own let go, its context deleted (seg1 `89aa`).

### Working the region out

[[read out]] `USER.EXE` seg1 `1582`, for a window and flags:

1. Before there is a desktop, the screen.
2. Nothing where the window or an ancestor is hidden (`WS_VISIBLE`) or an ancestor minimized; where the window itself is minimized, nothing for its client area if its class has an icon, and its whole rectangle -- its icon -- with `DCX_WINDOW` (`1c5f`-`1c94`). Nothing where it is the window [[fn:USER.LockWindowUpdate]] locked or within it and `DCX_LOCKWINDOWUPDATE` is not given (`15c4`-`15df`, `1c12`).
3. The window's client rectangle, or its window rectangle with `DCX_WINDOW` (`16b7`).
4. With `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN`, each visible child's window rectangle taken out, but not one with `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` (`162c`-`1656`, `16f2`: a child is taken out only where its rectangle meets the region's box, which comes to the same).
5. Then up the parents: at each level, with `DCX_CLIPSIBLINGS` for the first and the level's own `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` after, each visible sibling above taken out the same way; then cut to the parent's client rectangle (`1658`-`16ad`, `1774`). The desktop's client rectangle is the screen. A level whose region is empty ends the walk (`1687`, `1699`).

[[measured]] [[probe:visrgn]] holds each case to the pixel: a child without `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` keeps the whole of its client area under a sibling over it, one with it loses the part; a parent without `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` keeps its children's part, and `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` or `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` takes it out; `GetWindowDC` of a `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` window does not; `CS_PARENTDC` and `DCX_PARENTCLIP` give the parent's client area at the child's origin, but `GetWindowDC` and a plain `DCX_CACHE` the child's own; a pop-up partly off the screen and partly under another loses both parts; a hidden window has nothing; [[fn:USER.BeginPaint]]'s region is the client area within the update region, two rectangles apart staying apart. Top-level windows have `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` whether or not it was asked for.

[[measured]] [[probe:visrule]] holds the corners: `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` at each depth takes out only the window's own children, not a `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` one; a `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` child under a transparent sibling keeps that part; a grandchild under its parent's sibling keeps it where the parent has no `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` and loses it where it has; `GetWindowDC` of a `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` child loses its sibling above, and `DCX_CACHE` alone does not; a window at the top asked for with `DCX_CACHE` alone keeps what lies over it; a minimized window whose class has an icon has nothing for its client area and its icon for its whole rectangle, its child nothing either way; a child of a hidden window nothing; while a window is locked, it and its children have nothing, `DCX_LOCKWINDOWUPDATE` giving each its region, and another window is as it was.

A new memory context's visible region is empty; with a bitmap selected, the bitmap.

### When it is worked out again

- [[read out]] seg1 `8aa0`, given a window and a mode: every entry of that window and of its descendants is made stale. Mode 1, a window shown or hidden, makes it the parent's where the parent has `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` (mode 4: all but the parent's own `DCX_WINDOW` entries) or the window has `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` (mode 2: all but the parent's own), else the window's alone (`8ab1`-`8adc`). `SetWindowPos` and `ScrollWindow` give the parent, 4 or 2 by its `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` (seg7 `0585`-`0595`, seg13 `1656`-`1666`). As each entry of a window's own or a class's goes by, its `DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` is set by its window's style (`8af1`-`8b0a`). A free common entry is emptied, to be worked out when next taken (`8b4f`-`8b7d`); one in use, or a window's own, gets its flags from the window's style again and `SetHookFlags(DCHF_INVALIDATEVISRGN)` (`8b7f`-`8ba9`). Its callers: `SetWindowPos` (seg7 `0595`), a window shown or hidden (seg1 `56ef`, `5726`), a style's `WS_CLIPCHILDREN` or `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` changed (seg2 `0bce`), `LockWindowUpdate` and its end (seg13 `22dd`, `2306`), `ScrollWindow` moving children (seg13 `1666`), a window given `WS_CLIPSIBLINGS` (seg14 `0491`), and the desktop shown as Windows starts (seg3 `1ad5`).
- [[measured]] [[probe:dcentry]], `stale`: a parent with `WS_CLIPCHILDREN`'s `DCX_WINDOW | DCX_CACHE | DCX_CLIPCHILDREN` context, given back, its child moved by `SetWindowPos`, and asked for again: the same context, **its region still with the hole where the child was** -- `SetWindowPos` made the parent's entries stale but its whole-window ones -- until `SetHookFlags(DCHF_INVALIDATEVISRGN)` has USER's hook work it out with the child where it is. The parent's client area's context is worked out again after the child moves back.
- [[read out]] [[fn:USER.DCHook]] (seg1 `8a00`), which GDI calls the next time the context is used: for `DCHC_INVALIDVISRGN` it clears the flag, and for an entry in use works the region out again, applies the region given to `GetDCEx` again, selects it, and sets the origin. It answers 1, and does nothing for any other code.
- [[read out]] GDI calls a context's hook, where the flag is set, from the calls that use the context and its state: the drawing calls, the questions (`GetDCOrg`, `GetTextColor` and the rest through seg1 `5130`-`5193`), `SelectObject` (`1b32`), the visible-region family, `SetDCOrg`, `SetDCState` (`067f`); not from the calls that set one attribute and do no more -- `SetBkColor` and `SetTextColor` (seg1 `1476`, `14dc`, into `1526`), `SetBkMode`, `SetROP2`, `SetPolyFillMode`, `SetStretchBltMode`, `SetTextAlign`, `SetRelAbs` (`4e22`-`4ed8`, into `51a6`). [[measured]] [[probe:dchook]]: `GetClipBox`, `GetTextColor` and `Rectangle` each call a program's hook once, and again while the flag stays set. [[measured]] A program that selects a region of its own and sets the flag gets the window's region back; a region it saved is still there to restore ([[probe:visfam]], `hook`). [[measured]] A `CS_OWNDC` context kept while its window moves, and while another window comes over it and goes, answers for each where it is now ([[probe:visrgn]], `own`).

### USER's own uses

[[read out]] Besides `GetDCEx`, USER sets regions itself: `SetWindowPos` works out what of a moved window to copy (seg7 `0c41`) and selects it into a context for the copy (seg7 `0732`, `0975`); menus keep an item's drawing inside the bar with `SaveVisRgn`, `IntersectVisRect` and `RestoreVisRgn` (seg10 `29c3`-`2a30`, `2d70`-`2ec0`); `ScrollDC` and `ScrollWindow` blit through a region of their own and work out what was uncovered from `InquireVisRgn` and `GetDCOrg` (seg13 `1d90`-`1e5a`); the task switcher draws a window's icon by moving a context's origin and region (seg23 `0180`-`02d4`). `FastWindowFrame` draws the sizing frame (seg1 `8d74`). `EnableOEMLayer` calls `Resurrection` and `DisableOEMLayer` `Death` (seg5 `017e`, `01ba`).

## What it costs Windows

[[measured]] [[probe:viscost]] times `BitBlt` and `PatBlt` of 16 by 16 in a window at a fixed 80,000 instructions a millisecond, with windows over it: a pop-up clear of the drawing, eight of them, and one over half of it. A region of many rectangles costs almost nothing -- about 300 instructions more for a `BitBlt` with eight windows over, against 21,700 -- and a drawing half covered costs less, its pixels fewer. See [[topic:timing]].

## In winbox.js today

- A window's context is a view of the whole screen from its origin -- its window's corner, or where [[fn:GDI.SetDCOrg]] put it -- reaching left of and above nought too (`DeviceBitmap.screenView`, `src/raster/device-bitmap.ts`; `DeviceBitmap::screen_view`, `crates/winbox-raster/src/device_bitmap.rs`). The raster's pixels there are its `reach`, not its width and height, which stay the window's. It draws through one region: the clip region within the visible region moved by the origin (`Surface`'s `dcClip`, `src/raster/surface.ts`; `regions::drawn_through` and `draw::canvas`, `crates/winbox-win16/src/gdi`). Phase 3, below.
- USER works each window context's visible region out by Windows' rule (phase 2, below; `src/win16/user/visible-region-rule.ts`, `crates/winbox-win16/src/window_vis.rs`), and GDI keeps it in the context, in the screen's terms, with its stack and its origin (phase 1). `GetClipBox`, `RectVisible`, `PtVisible` and `GetPixel` ask the clip region within it (`clipOf`, `src/win16/gdi/clipping.ts`; `clip_of`, `crates/winbox-win16/src/gdi/regions.rs`).
- USER's own drawing -- frames, icons, controls, menus, the caret where the window has no context of its own -- goes through views of the window's own rectangle, each kept to the window's region by the same rule, `DCX_USESTYLE`, with `DCX_WINDOW` for the whole window, and while the window paints, to what it paints (`#view` in `src/win16/user/desktop.ts`; `window_part` in `crates/winbox-win16/src/surface.rs`).
- Each pixel of the screen still keeps the window that shows there (`owners`, `#own`; `desktop.rs`), for finding the window at a point and what a move uncovers; nothing draws by it.
- The clip region a program sets is in the context's own terms, intersected with the visible region moved by the origin.
- [[fn:USER.GetDCEx]] (`src/win16/user/get-dc-ex.ts`, `crates/winbox-win16/src/get_dc.rs`) works the region out by its flags, `DCX_INTERSECTRGN` and `DCX_EXCLUDERGN` in it. `DCX_PARENTCLIP` and `CS_PARENTDC` give the parent's region, which the clip box answers and drawing goes through, outside the window's own client area too ([[probe:visdraw]]).
- USER's device context entries are kept as Windows keeps them (`src/win16/user/dc-entries.ts`, `crates/winbox-win16/src/dc_entries.rs`; phase 4, below): five common ones made at the start, each a context of its own with its own surface and attributes, and one for each `CS_OWNDC` window -- the window's own surface -- and each `CS_CLASSDC` class. `GetDC`, `GetWindowDC`, `GetDCEx` and `BeginPaint` take one as seg1 `847b` does, `ReleaseDC` and `EndPaint` give it back as `8805` does, and windows changing make entries stale as `8aa0` does; GDI calls a context's hook where its flag is set (`dc-hook-calls.ts`, `gdi/dc_hook_calls.rs`), USER's run by WinBox itself.

## The design

### The model

- **GDI keeps the visible region in the context, in the screen's terms**, as Windows does: `visRegion` on the TypeScript engine's `Surface`, `vis` on the Rust engine's `Dc`, with the stack `SaveVisRgn` pushes and the origin. A memory context's is its bitmap's rectangle (empty for the one-pixel bitmap); a printer's, its page and the band (`printer-bands.ts`'s band moves into it); the screen's, the screen.
- **One region to draw through.** The context keeps what Windows keeps at `1Eh`: the visible region moved to the context's own terms by the origin, intersected with the clip region. It is made again when either changes, and it is the one test the raster makes on a pixel (`dcClip`), in place of the view's test and the clip region's. `GetClipBox`, `RectVisible`, `PtVisible` and every drawing call ask it.
- **The clip region stays in the context's own terms.** Windows keeps it in the screen's and moves it with the origin, which comes to the same thing; `GetClipRgn` adds the origin to what it answers.
- **USER works the region out**, with Windows' rule (`USER.EXE` seg1 `1582`), from the window tree -- rectangles, styles, the order of siblings, what is shown and minimized, `LockWindowUpdate` -- not from `owners`, whose rule is another. `owners` stays for what it is also used for: finding the window at a point, and what a move uncovers.
- **A window's context is a view of the whole screen from its origin**, drawing at any pixel of the screen the region allows: the raster's views take a rectangle of addresses that may start left of and above nought (`IndexedContext.address`, `indexed_context.rs`), not a width and height from nought. Then `DCX_PARENTCLIP`, `CS_PARENTDC`, a program's `SelectVisRgn` reaching out of its window, and `SetDCOrg` all draw where Windows draws.
- **USER's hook is called by GDI.** A context USER hands out carries USER's `DCHook` and the flag; when windows change, USER sets the flag on the contexts concerned, and GDI calls the hook before it next uses one. The hook is WinBox's own code; a program's own hook is called through the processor, as [[probe:dchook]] records.

### The risks

- **Time on the host.** On the development machine the Rust engine's raster spends 6 ns on a pixel drawn with no test, 8 with one test -- the window's pixels or a region alike -- 9.5 with a region of eight holes, and 11.3 with both of today's tests. One region in place of two is a little faster. The TypeScript engine spends about 570 ns on a pixel, and its tests are lost in it. Working a region out is a few region operations for each context handed out, and once more for each context made stale. Neither needs a test of its own; drawing a run of a row at a time against the region's bands, rather than a pixel at a time, is a later gain open to both.
- **SimTower's clock.** The faithful clock charges a call what Windows ran for it, whatever winbox.js does inside; [[probe:viscost]] shows a region's rectangles add next to nothing in Windows, so nothing is to be charged for them. USER's hook is WinBox's own code and runs no instructions; only a program's own hook would. SimTower makes none of the family's calls, nor `GetClipBox` nor `RectVisible`. Predicted unchanged at every phase; checked with the faithful-clock survey.
- **The two engines.** Every phase lands in both, as twins; the Rust engine's stubs answering what was in AX go in phase 1.
- **USER's own drawing** into windows -- frames, menus, controls -- goes through the same contexts and changes with them in phase 3; the probes of [[topic:painting]] and [[topic:window-frames]] are the check. As built, it goes through views of its own kept to the same rule, and no probe of either moved.

### What stays out

- `Death` and `Resurrection` turning anything off: WinBox runs no DOS program full screen and has no display driver to turn off. They answer what the display's driver would (decision 4).
- A program changing the region `InquireVisRgn` gave it, behind GDI's back: not recorded.
- Charging a drawing call by how much of it shows ([[probe:viscost]]'s half-covered drawing costs Windows a tenth less).
- The task switcher's and the menus' own uses: WinBox's switcher and menus are its own.

### Decisions

The owner's, 2026-10-26:

1. Phase 2 lands on its own, each changed `GetClipBox` or `RectVisible` answer of the corpus explained.
2. A window's context becomes a view of the whole screen from its origin, negative coordinates allowed, as Windows has it -- phase 3's work. Nothing of phase 1 contradicts it: the origin and the visible region are kept in the screen's terms, apart from the view.
3. Phase 4 gives each of a window's contexts a drawing surface of its own, as Windows has, not records over the one surface.
4. `Death` and `Resurrection` answer what the display driver's `Disable` and `Enable` return, read out of the drivers the oracle uses (above), without turning WinBox's display off; where a driver's answer depends on state, that is said.

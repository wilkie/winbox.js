## Observed behaviour

- [[measured]] [[probe:prtteng]]'s driver answers [[fn:GDI.GetTextExtent]] with it, the count negative. `abcdefgh` of Arial at an em of 40 is 163 across, the sum of its widths, and 45 down, the font's cell. A character outside the font is the default character's width. With a character extra of 3, 24 more.
- [[measured]] Arial turned 90 degrees is 44 down, the turned font's cell.
- [[measured]] [[probe:prttxt]]: given a count of nought or more, GDI calls the driver's own `ExtTextOut` again, with the same device, place and string but a font of its own -- a raster font of version 3.0, its `FONTINFO` at 42h of its segment, of type 3, characters 1Eh to FFh, the default character 1Fh and the break 20h, the cell the TrueType font's (27 high for Arial at an em of 24, its ascent 21). Each of the string's characters has its glyph there, its width the glyph's ink, not its advance. Its `dfBitsOffset` is A66h for Arial at 24, A36h for Arial bold at 18 and A3Eh for Wingdings at 20.
- [[measured]] With that call come the widths of each character, its advance -- 17 and 14 for Arial's `Ab` at that size -- and the `DRAWMODE`'s background as transparent.
- [[measured]] The answer is the `PDEVICE` GDI handed the driver, in every record: DX the `PDEVICE`'s selector, which was 2927h, AX nought.
- [[measured]] `ExtTextOut` with `ETO_OPAQUE` and an opaque rectangle comes as two calls: a count of nought with the options `ETO_OPAQUE | ETO_CLIPPED` and the background opaque, which fills the rectangle; then the string with no options and the background transparent. Widths a program gives come changed, by a pixel here and there to the glyphs' own places.
- [[measured]] An italic font GDI slants itself comes with the string moved right, three pixels at an em of 20.
- [[measured]] A turned string never reaches the driver's `ExtTextOut`: GDI draws each glyph through the driver's `BitBlt`, raster operation E20746h, the glyph's bitmap the source, cut to the device -- Arial's `Ab` turned 90 degrees at 10,10 is one blit of the `A`, 17 by 10 at 15,0 from 0,5 of the glyph, the `b` falling above the device.
- [[measured]] The driver draws GDI's font with [[fn:GDI.DMExtTextOut]] onto a band of its own; the band's bits are recorded.
- [[measured]] [[probe:prttx2]]: a turned string on an opaque background is preceded by a polygon the driver's `Output` fills, style 20 (`OS_WINDPOLYGON`): `Ab` of Arial at an em of 24 turned 90 degrees at 30,40 is 30,40 30,11 57,11 57,40 -- 29 along the baseline, the string's extent, and 27 across, the cell -- and at 30 degrees 30,40 55,25 69,48 44,63. With `ETO_OPAQUE` and a rectangle, the rectangle is filled after it with `PATCOPY` (F00021h) through the driver's `BitBlt`, then the glyphs are blitted.
- [[measured]] [[probe:prttstk]]: the string is cut to what the stack holds. With the task's stack limit, the word at 0Ah of the stack's segment, put a margin below the stack pointer as the driver calls, sixteen characters come to the driver as none at a margin of 4FBh to 4FEh, one at 4FFh, and one more for each 4 bytes after, all sixteen from 53Bh; under 4FBh nothing comes at all. The answer is the `PDEVICE` still.
- [[measured]] A context made by `CreateDC` with the driver named `MGXWMF.DRV` -- in any case, `mgxwmf.drv` too -- has its string's widths the advances alone and its place not moved by the first A space: `jAWj` of Arial at an em of 24 at 10,10 comes as 6, 15, 23, 6 at 10,10, where the same driver named `MGXWMF` is given 7, 15, 22, 6 at 9,10.
- [[measured]] For a driver without `RC_GDI20_OUTPUT` (its device "Plain"), the string comes to the driver's `StrBlt` a character at a time: `xAy` at 10,10 is three calls, at 10, 21 and 37, each of count 1, the font at 42h of the cache's segment and the clip the one given -- cut to the opaque rectangle for `ETO_CLIPPED`. An opaque background is the driver's `BitBlt` with `PATCOPY`: the text's box, 29 by 27 for `Ab`; given `ETO_OPAQUE` and a rectangle, the rectangle twice, before the characters. A turned string goes glyph by glyph through `BitBlt` as for any driver, after its polygon.

## Nuances

- The width the driver is given for a glyph is its advance moved by the difference of its A space and the next one's, so that a driver drawing the ink one after another puts each where its outline would be; the string starts at the first glyph's A space past the place. A turned string's widths are the advances alone.
- The font GDI hands back is the context's own realized `FONTINFO`'s, not the one the driver passed.

## Inside Windows

[[read out]] `GDI.EXE` seg1 `669a`. It takes 42 bytes: the device context, then a driver's `ExtTextOut` arguments — the device, the place, the clip, the string, the count, the `FONTINFO`, the `DRAWMODE`, the `TEXTXFORM`, the widths, the opaque rectangle and the options. It answers a doubleword.

- The `FONTINFO`'s block at 54h is made again where it was discarded (`6748`, into seg3 `2f5e`); nought answered where it cannot be.
- For a negative count, the string's extent (`69fe`, into `6a65`), measured with the context's own realized font (`6b73`). Each character's width is the one [[fn:GDI.EngineGetCharWidth]] gives, the context's character extra after each, its justification spread as [[fn:GDI.GetTextExtent]] spreads it, and the cell down. An extent past 7FFFh is 7FFFh. A font whose widths could not be filled answers nought across and 8000h down. A turned font's extent is stretched where GDI's two pixel shapes differ.
- Otherwise the context's device, its word 2Ah, is made the one given, and its flag 1 at 0Ah set, while GDI draws the string itself (`6223`); then both are put back, and the device as it was is the answer (`672b`-`6740`) -- whatever the drawing came to, the drawing's own answer, nought and 8000h where it fails, thrown away. That is the DX 2927h of the records: the selector of the probe's `PDEVICE`.

[[read out]] The cache block (seg3 `2f2a`, `2fd3`). For a `FONTINFO` flagged 80h at 33h, which every one the engine realizes is, a raster font file of version 3.0: its character table at 94h, six bytes an entry; else 2.0, at 76h, four. Its two widths-filled flags at 33h are cleared. After the table, one entry more than the characters, come a word for each character's advance, a word of its A and C spaces, 20h bytes for the face's name, and the rasterizer's scratch -- the widest glyph's bytes a row, `(dfMaxWidth + 3Fh) / 8` with its low two bits cleared, times the cell; none for a turned font -- and then the glyphs. The block is `GlobalAlloc(2042h)` of that and a glyph area more: the average glyph's bytes times 20 under 20 points, 15 under 35, 10 under 60, 5 under 90, 3 under 150, else once, and 400h at least. Its header: the version, the size, the copyright `MicroSoft (c) 1990 - This is an awesome engine font!`, then the `FONTINFO`'s first 23h bytes with bit 0 of the pitch set, `dfFace` the name's place, `dfBitsOffset` the glyphs'. The first character, 1Eh, is 8 wide, its glyph the scratch. In the `FONTINFO`: the handle at 54h, the scratch and its size at 56h and 58h, the tables at 5Ah and 5Ch, the size at 5Eh, the glyph area at 62h, the next glyph's place at 66h and what is left at 6Ah -- those two written at 66h and 6Ah of the `FONTINFO`'s segment, its `FONTINFO` starting a block of its own.

[[read out]] GDI's drawing of a TrueType string, seg1 `6223`: with TrueType off (`[064Ah]` bit nought clear), or a font flagged 40h at 33h, nothing. The glyphs of the string are made ready in the font's cache (`7010`): each character of the string without a glyph has it made by the rasterizer (`70b3`, into `7222`), a character outside the font the second's, the default; the engine's context set to the font at its angle once (`74d7`). An upright glyph's entry has its width, at least 1, and its place; its bytes are laid a column of the cell at a time, its top row at the ascent less the glyph's top, what falls outside the cell left off; its advance goes in the widths, its left and what the advance leaves past its right in the spaces (`7300`-`7404`). A turned glyph is kept as the rasterizer made it, a row a doubleword's multiple, after 12 bytes of its width, height and corner (`752d`). A block too small grows by its glyph area (`71a6`), past 64K only for version 3.0; one that cannot grow is made again once (`71fd`). A glyph the rasterizer fails is the default's (`70ec`). Then the cache's `dfBitsPointer` set from its `dfBitsOffset` and the font answered as the cache's segment and 42h (`7187`-`719a`).

[[read out]] The widths, `6874`: a font of fixed pitch with nothing more its pitch; the widths given where there is nothing else; else the cache's advances with the character extra (and a pixel for a bold the device's `dpText` makes, `TC_EA_DOUBLE` in the `TEXTXFORM` too, `6b73`), or, with breaks or negative spacing, each character's own (`6ba7`), a break's share of the justification spread by its error term, which is kept in the context. Upright, each width is moved by the next glyph's A space less its own (`6986`).

[[read out]] The calls, `637f`-`656b`, through the context's table, entry 34h, its `ExtTextOut` (`65be`-`663a`), with GDI's copy of the `DRAWMODE` -- its justification and character extra nought -- and the cache's font. Which arguments go is a flag byte: the count or nought, GDI's widths or none, the opaque rectangle given or the text's own box, and the options given, given less `ETO_OPAQUE`, or 6. No characters: one call with what was given. A driver whose `dpRaster` has `RC_OP_DX_OUTPUT` (4000h) draws an opaque background in its one call. Otherwise an opaque background is a call of no characters filling the rectangle, the options 6, where `ETO_OPAQUE` and a rectangle are given (`6521`); one filling the text's box where the rectangle does not hold it, cut to it for `ETO_CLIPPED` (`6548`); then the string, transparent. A driver without `RC_GDI20_OUTPUT` (10h), and a font GDI draws as an outline (20h at 33h), go by seg8 `00e4`: GDI's own fill and the driver's `StrBlt` a character at a time.

[[read out]] A turned font (flag 10h at 33h) goes by seg8 `024b`: a brush of the text colour selected, the text white and the background black (`002d`, `0268`); the first baseline point the place moved by the ascent, turned by GDI's own sine and cosine (seg33 `00b6`, `00fb`: a table of each degree's sine times 10000, the tenths between taken in proportion, `MulDiv` each), across by the resolutions' ratio; each glyph at the baseline point of the widths before it, moved by its corner, through seg1 `2ef2`: the driver's `BitBlt` for each rectangle of the clip within the glyph's box, top down, the source a monochrome `BITMAP` of the glyph -- its width taken up to 32 -- and the raster operation of the context's `ROP2` from a table at seg8 `020b`, E20746h for `R2_COPYPEN`. An opaque background is filled first (`63ea`-`64be`): a brush of the background colour and a null pen selected (seg8 `0023`, `0004`), and the turned box filled through GDI's own `Polygon` in the device's terms, `WINDING` (seg24 `0396`, given 2): the place; the place carried along the baseline by the string's extent -- its widths and the two A spaces 6874 answers, past 7FFFh cut to 7FFFh less the place -- across by its cosine and up by its sine of the extent times the vertical-to-horizontal ratio (`[bp-52h]`, `256 * V / H` rounded, `625b`-`6288`); that point and the place carried down the cell, across by the sine of the cell times the other ratio and down by its cosine. Then, where options and an opaque rectangle are given, the rectangle filled with `PATCOPY` through `2ef2`, the clip's rectangles within it (`6482`-`64bb`); the brush and pen put back and deleted (`6585`).

[[read out]] GDI's own `ExtTextOut` on a driver's context draws a turned engine font itself, without the driver's `ExtTextOut` (seg1 `3a1a`-`3a86`, into `620e`, which makes the block and goes to `6223`): where the device's `GDIINFO` word 5Eh has no bit 2, the context's flag 4 at 0Bh is set, the realized font is of type 3 and turned, and there are characters. [[measured]] So [[probe:prttxt]]'s driver logs no `engine` for a turned string, only the blits. [[read out]] The flag is the selected font's kind. Selecting a font (seg1 `3ff9`-`4094`) clears flags 4, 8 and 10h at 0Bh and sets one by the font mapper's answer: 4 for 539h, a device font -- the `FONTINFO` the driver's `RealizeObject` made, scored against GDI's own fonts and found better (seg3 `0740`-`080b`, and `13c1`) -- 10h for 53Ah, a TrueType font GDI's engine realized (seg3 `142a`, `2af2`), and 8 for some of GDI's vector fonts, by the device's `dpText` (`401a`-`404d`). seg3 `0261`-`0272` tells the same three apart. So a turned engine font a driver realized with `EngineRealizeFont` is drawn by GDI, and one GDI realized itself goes through GDI's own text, not this test; WinBox's driver path is only ever given the first.

[[read out]] A driver without `RC_GDI20_OUTPUT`, and a font GDI draws as an outline, are called through seg8 `00e4` instead of the driver's `ExtTextOut` -- the near procedure at `621f` rather than `6221` (`62e1`-`62f2`) -- for every call the flag byte plans. A call that is not the string's (its bits 78h not all set) fills: the opaque rectangle given where bit 80h is set, else the text's box, cut to the clip (`663e`), through the driver's `BitBlt` with `PATCOPY`, the context's brush a solid one of the background colour for it (`0065`-`00c8`). The string's call fills the opaque rectangle the same way where `ETO_OPAQUE` is among the options (`00fa`) -- a second time, where a call before it filled it -- cuts the clip to the rectangle for `ETO_CLIPPED`, drawing nothing where nothing is left (`0109`-`012a`), and hands the driver's `StrBlt` each character with a count of 1 (`016d`-`01c5`): the first place the place moved by the ascent at 8 of the `FONTINFO` turned -- across by its sine times `256 * H / V`, up by what the ascent's cosine leaves of it -- and each character at the widths before it (`01c7`, a running total written over them) carried along the baseline, through GDI's sines (`01e1`, `01f0`); the clip, the cache's font, GDI's copy of the `DRAWMODE` with the background transparent and the `TEXTXFORM` given. A font GDI draws as an outline goes to its own drawing at `042a` instead.

[[read out]] The count is cut to what GDI's stack holds as it starts: the stack pointer less the task's stack limit at `[SS:000Ah]` -- nothing at all under 400h, the drawing answering nought and 8000h -- less 400h, shifted right two with its sign, the most characters, compared unsigned (`62a5`-`62c0`). From `EngineExtTextOut`'s return address to there the stack holds CEh bytes: its `enter 6` and two words (`669d`-`66a4`), the 20 words it pushes and the near call (`66f0`-`6728`), and `6223`'s `enter 90h` and three words. [[measured]] [[probe:prttstk]]'s margins, as the driver's code reads its stack pointer, are FBh more than the room GDI measures: 4FBh is 400h exactly, the CEh and 2Dh of the driver's own call.

[[read out]] The string counts as turned -- its widths the advances alone, the first A space not taken (`6874` given the count negative, `6986`) -- for a turned font, and for a context flagged 40h at 0Bh (`62d0`). `CreateDC` sets the flag (seg1 `0242`-`024f`) where the atom of the driver's name (`0093`) is `[014Dh]`: the atom GDI adds for `MGXWMF.DRV` as it starts (seg2 `024e`-`0259`, through seg1 `210e`, `AddAtom` in GDI's own atom table, so without case), beside `[014Fh]`, `DISPLAY`'s. It is the only use of the flag in `EngineExtTextOut`; the name is Micrografx's metafile driver's. [[measured]] [[probe:prttstk]]: GDI finds the driver named `MGXWMF.DRV` by `LoadLibrary` of `MGXWMF.DRV.drv`, which `KERNEL` answers with the module `MGXWMF` already loaded.

## Implementation

`crates/winbox-win16/src/gdi/engine_glyphs.rs`: the extent, with the `FONTINFO` given, which a driver hands on from its own `ExtTextOut`. `crates/winbox-win16/src/gdi/engine_text.rs`: the drawing, the cache laid out from named constants as GDI lays it, its glyphs the rasterizer's ([[fn:GDI.EngineGetGlyphBmp]]'s). The turned string's opaque background and the driver's `StrBlt` path for a driver without `RC_GDI20_OUTPUT` are drawn as read out (`turnedGround`, `byStrBlt`). The count is cut as read out where the stack pointer is known, `EngineExtTextOut`'s (`stackCut`, `stack_cut`); a context of a driver named `MGXWMF.DRV` is flagged as `CreateDC` flags it. [[probe:prttxt]], [[probe:prttx2]] and [[probe:prttstk]] agree in every record.

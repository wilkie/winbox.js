## Observed behaviour

- [[measured]] [[probe:kowner]]: a moveable block made USER's, then its prefix's again: [[fn:KERNEL.K404]] answers each owner set. AX comes back as the caller had it, not the selector. The block is freed after with `GlobalFree`, nought.

## Inside Windows

- [[read out]] `KRNL386.EXE` seg1 `29f1`-`2a00`. Two words, a selector and the new owner, four bytes popped (the selector is the first pushed, at BP+8). It calls seg1 `2a03`, which finds the block's arena as [[fn:KERNEL.K404]] does and writes the owner to its word at 12h (`2a13`-`2a1c`), EAX pushed and popped about it (`2a11`, `2a20`): nothing is answered.
- [[read out]] `KRNL286.EXE` seg1 `26a9` writes the word at 1 of the arena a paragraph before the block (seg1 `26bc`-`26cb`).
- [[read out]] KERNEL uses it while it makes a task. The new task's database is made its own owner: seg2 `2160`-`2164` calls `29f1` with the database's selector as both arguments.

## Callers

- No file of the installations or the corpus imports it or holds its name (`kb/data/kernel-callers.json`).

## Implementation

In WinBox, (`crates/winbox-win16/src/kernel_calls/owners.rs`), nothing answered. The owner is kept for [[fn:KERNEL.K404]] while the block is there. A block of `GlobalAlloc`'s made a task's prefix's is that task's, as its own are, and freed with them at its end or by [[fn:KERNEL.GlobalFreeAll]]; made anything else's it is no task's, so a task's end leaves it, as Windows frees only the blocks the task's prefix owns. Not measured: a block made another's and its task ended.

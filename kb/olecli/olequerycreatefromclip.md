## Observed behaviour

- [[measured]] [[probe:olestat]]: `Static` drawn is `OLE_OK` with a picture, a bitmap or a DIB there; with no rendering `OLE_ERROR_FORMAT`; in a format there `OLE_OK`. `StdFileEditing` with only those `OLE_ERROR_FORMAT`; a protocol neither `OLE_ERROR_PROTOCOL` (15).

## Nuances

- [[measured]] [[probe:oledde]], [[probe:olepbd]], [[probe:olepbr]]: an embedded or linked object -- `StdFileEditing`, a server talked to by DDE -- is WinBox's own, with `OLECLI.DLL` on the drive and without it: its `LE` method answers ([[topic:ole]], "Embedded and linked objects").

## Implementation

WinBox's own: `OleQueryCreateFromClip` in `crates/winbox-win16/src/olecli/api.rs`, written from the interface and the probes, not from the code of `OLECLI.DLL` ([[topic:ole]]). WinBox agrees with [[probe:olestat]], [[probe:olemem]], [[probe:oledde]], [[probe:olepbd]] and [[probe:olepbr]], with `OLECLI.DLL` on the drive and without it (`crates/winbox-win16/tests/olecli.rs`).
